Connect opencode

Developers use an unmodified opencode 2.3.x. One device login, and Wrench provides the rest.

Install

  • macOS: brew install opencode
  • Linux or Windows: the v2.3.0 archive from opencode releases

npm's opencode-ai package is the 1.x line and does not work with Wrench.

Sign in

opencode auth login opencode --method device --answer server=https://wrench.example.com

opencode opens a page with a code. Sign in with GitLab if asked, check that the code matches and press Approve. Sign out with opencode auth logout.

What you get

  • Models: your tier's models. Other providers are blocked, even with their keys in your environment.
  • /usage: your 5-hour, day, week and month budgets.
  • Sessions and device keys on the dashboard, where you can revoke a key.

Turn off opencode's /share: it uploads conversations to opencode.ai.
